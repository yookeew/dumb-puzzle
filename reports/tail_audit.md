## 2. Is the label self-consistent?

- `TAXITIME_SEC_mvt == MVT_TIME − BLOCK_TIME` (±2 s): **100.0%** of rows. The label is the block-derived taxi by construction; a long taxi means BLOCK_TIME is long before takeoff.

- taxi ≥    0 s (n=339,014, AOBT_3 present): median `taxi − (MVT−AOBT_3)` = **-53 s**; 239 rows have MVT−AOBT_3 < 0
- taxi ≥ 1500 s (n=34,754, AOBT_3 present): median `taxi − (MVT−AOBT_3)` = **361 s**; 58 rows have MVT−AOBT_3 < 0
- taxi ≥ 2400 s (n=4,570, AOBT_3 present): median `taxi − (MVT−AOBT_3)` = **1385 s**; 24 rows have MVT−AOBT_3 < 0
- taxi ≥ 3600 s (n=774, AOBT_3 present): median `taxi − (MVT−AOBT_3)` = **3176 s**; 6 rows have MVT−AOBT_3 < 0

A large positive gap means the recorded taxi is inflated relative to the NM actual off-block — the block feed pushed the off-block earlier (schedule echo / ATFM absorption), it is not extra time on the tarmac.

- echo rows (|BLOCK−SCHED| < 30 s): 17,308 (5.0%), mean taxi 1145 s vs 1000 s non-echo.
